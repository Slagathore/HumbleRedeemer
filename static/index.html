Privacy

Everything runs and stays on your machine. Your logins are kept as session cookies in local files (.humblecookies, .steamcookies, .gogcookies), your keys and history live in a local database (redeemer.db), and the only servers this app ever contacts are Humble Bundle, Steam, and GOG — acting as you, for you — plus a read-only version check against this project's GitHub page at launch (it downloads public repo info; it never sends anything about you). No telemetry, no analytics, no accounts, no data collection of any kind.

Disclaimer: unofficial tool, not affiliated with Humble Bundle, Valve, or GOG. Automating your accounts may conflict with those services' terms — use at your own risk, no warranty. You are responsible for your own accounts and keys.
