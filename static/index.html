Spare keys you can give away

Steam keys for games you already own where the key itself was never used. Keys refused with Steam's "already owned" error are still valid for other people. For unrevealed spares, Make gift link creates a Humble gift link — the nicest way to hand one out. Entries marked verify first came from old runs where we can't tell "you already owned it" apart from "someone else used this key" — test those before promising them. The Ownership column traces how you came to own each game (run Verify licenses to fill it): not from a key means this key can't be the one that gave you the game, while could be this key means a product key created that ownership and no record explains which one.

Likely redeemed already

Steam's license history says these games entered your account through a product key, and no recorded redemption explains which one. Since you held this Humble key before the app started tracking, odds are the "spare" below IS the key that got you the game. Privacy

Everything runs and stays on your machine. Your logins are kept as session cookies in local files (.humblecookies, .steamcookies), your keys and history live in a local database (redeemer.db), and the only servers this app ever contacts are Humble Bundle and Steam — acting as you, for you — plus a read-only version check against this project's GitHub page at launch (it downloads public repo info; it never sends anything about you). No telemetry, no analytics, no accounts, no data collection of any kind.

Disclaimer: unofficial tool, not affiliated with Humble Bundle, Valve, or GOG. Automating your accounts may conflict with those services' terms — use at your own risk, no warranty. You are responsible for your own accounts and keys.

Market auto-confirm (advanced)

Selling on the Steam market needs a mobile confirmation for each listing. Without this you approve them by hand in the Steam app; with your authenticator's identity_secret saved here, the app confirms its own listings. You can't get this from the stock Steam app — only from Steam Desktop Authenticator (SDA) or an extracted maFile. It's stored locally in .steamguard, the same trust level as your saved session; it can't be hashed because the app has to use it to sign each confirmation. Leave this blank to just confirm on your phone.

Add keys to give away

One per line: game name, then the key or link. :, |, a dash, or just a space all work as separators, e.g.

These are your own outside keys. They skip matching and redemption entirely, live in the good-to-go list (tagged "added by you"), and can be rolled, emailed, and marked given like any other spare. Nothing is added if any line can't be parsed.
